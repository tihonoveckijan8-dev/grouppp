# Audio credits — BandPlan guitar tuner

The guitar tuner reference tones use three recorded acoustic-guitar samples from the **tonejs-instruments** collection by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky and contributors:

- Source project: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
- Pinned source revision: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/tree/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/samples/guitar-acoustic
- Sample files: `A2.mp3`, `A3.mp3`, `A4.mp3`
- Original instrument sample source listed by upstream: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios
- Sample license declared by upstream: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Attribution: Instrument samples from the `nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments` collection, licensed CC BY 3.0. Credit to the tonejs-instruments contributors and the original sample authors catalogued in upstream `sample-source-info.txt`, including the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.

The app pitch-shifts these recordings to the six standard guitar open-string frequencies. If the audio samples cannot be loaded, the tuner uses its local synthesized fallback so the control remains usable.
